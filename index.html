About

Pioneering Nanotechnology for a Sustainable World

Prof. Vineet Kumar is a Professor in the Department of Biotechnology at Lovely Professional University, Punjab, India. With over 17 years of research and teaching experience, his work bridges the intersection of nanotechnology, biotechnology, and environmental science.

His research focuses on green synthesis of nanoparticles using plant extracts, biosensors for food and environmental monitoring, nanomedicine and drug delivery systems, and sustainable environmental remediation using nanomaterials. He has published more than 86 research and review articles with a total impact factor exceeding 200.

Beyond the laboratory, Prof. Kumar has mentored 6 PhD students, co-authored 17+ books and edited volumes with leading publishers including Springer, Elsevier, and CRC Press, and serves on editorial boards of prestigious journals including Scientific Reports (SpringerNature).

Research Focus

Areas of Expertise

⬡
Green Synthesis of Nanoparticles

Synthesis of silver, gold, MgO, MnO₂ and other metallic nanoparticles using plant extracts. Characterization and biocompatibility assessment for biomedical and environmental applications.

⬡
Nanomedicine & Drug Delivery

Development of PLGA nanoparticles for anticancer biomolecule delivery, biosurfactant-coated nanoparticles, and lignocellulose-based nanomaterials for therapeutic applications.

⬡
Nanosensors & Biosensors

Electrochemical and optical sensing platforms for food safety, environmental pollutants (heavy metals, dyes, pesticides), and disease diagnostics including DNA-nanosensors.

⬡
Environmental Remediation

Photocatalytic degradation of azo dyes, heavy metal removal using lignocellulosic nanomaterials, and wastewater treatment using advanced nanocomposites.

⬡
Plant Nanobiotechnology

Nanopriming effects on plant growth, antioxidant potential, and stress tolerance. MgO, TiO₂-Al₂O₃-ZnFe₂O₄ nanocomposites for sustainable agriculture.

⬡
Corrosion Science

Green corrosion inhibitors from plant extracts for steel used in biomedical and petroleum industries. Molecular dynamic simulation and quantum chemical calculations.
